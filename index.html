Questions

What is lake.world?

A water ski log for you and your ski club. Log your slalom sets and kneeboard tricks, and see how everyone in the club is doing on the club board.

What do I log for slalom?

Pick your line length, boat speed and buoy count, then submit your best run for the set. A faster boat at the same line counts as harder, so 4 buoys at 30 mph beats 6 at 28 mph. Speed shows in mph or kph.

What about kneeboard?

Search for a trick or write in your own, and build up the list of tricks you've landed.

How do club boards work?

Sign in, pick your club and ask to join. A club admin approves you before you can see the roster, so only members see who's on it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. lake.world runs in the browser on your phone or computer, so you can log a set from the dock.
