Slalom rope lengths chart

"Off" means feet shortened from a 75 ft (23 m) slalom line. So 28 off is a 47 ft rope, and 38 off is a 37 ft rope.

Rope lengths, 15 off to 44 off

The metre lengths and loop colours are from IWWF World Waterski Rules, Rule 5.09e, measured from the handle under tension. The "off" names are the ones used in the USA Water Ski & Wake Sports 2026 Official Tournament Rules (Rule 10.06.G). The feet column is simply 75 minus the "off" number, so it's the everyday name, not a tape-measure reading.

Rule 5.09e allows ±15 cm on the 18.25 m, 16 m and 14.25 m loops, and ±7.5 cm from 13 m on. The 9.50 m loop (44 off) is optional and may be a separate rope.

About the colours

Tournament ropes follow the IWWF colour sequence above, so a red loop on a tournament line is 15 off. Recreational and older ropes don't always match: colours vary by manufacturer, and some lines reuse a colour or leave a loop plain. Check the length on your own rope before you trust the colour.

How a slalom score reads

A score like 3 @ 35 off means the skier finished every pass through 32 off (skied or waived), then rounded 3 buoys at 35 off (a 40 ft, 12.00 m rope). Tournaments write the same pass in metres, as 3@12. You can turn any pass into an official buoy count with the slalom score calculator.
