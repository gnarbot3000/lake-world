                  FAQ

                  How do I log a slalom set?

                  Pick line, speed, and buoy count, then Submit this set’s best run.

                  How does Score rank work?

                  Faster boat at the same line is harder (4 @ 30 mph beats 6 @ 28 mph). After 36 mph, more off is harder, then buoys.

                  How does Beat average work?

                  Latest day versus the average of your two best Scores in the last 12 months (not counting today). Whoever beats their own average by more ranks higher. Score board stays raw ability.
