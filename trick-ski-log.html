How a trick run is scored

A tournament trick round is two passes through the trick course. As USA Water Ski & Wake Sports puts it, the skier has two 20-second passes, each trick has an assigned point value, and each trick can be done only once. The highest total wins. Beginners usually trick on two short skis, and intermediate and elite skiers on one.

The IWWF Waterski Rules 2026 (Rule 9) spell out how the points add up:

  • Every trick has a code and a value. The value depends on whether you're on two skis or one, and the reverse (the same trick turned the other way) has its own value.
  • No repeats. Each trick number scores once as a basic and once as a reverse in the run. Do it again and it scores zero.
  • Reverses go in the same pass. A reverse only scores if it comes right after its basic in the same pass, though 180s are allowed in between.
  • Land it to count it. You have to stay up and in skiing position, and a trick not finished before the end of the pass doesn't score.
  • Six flips max. Only six flips count in a run.
  • Your score is the sum. Add up every credited trick from both passes.

        What to write down after each run

        • Pass 1 and pass 2: the tricks you went for on each pass, in order.
        • Landed or missed: a trick only counts if you finish it without falling, so note which ones stuck.
        • New tricks: the first time you land something, mark it. These are the milestones you'll want to look back on.
        • Your total: run it through the calculator above and save it, so you can watch the number climb.
        • Conditions: boat speed, wind and water, so you can see what made a good run good.

        What the trick log does

        The trick log in lake.world is a checklist of tricks you've landed. Each time you land something new on a run, you mark it, and your trick list grows over the season. It's quick enough to fill in between runs at the dock.

        Built-in kneeboard tricks

        The built-in catalog is for kneeboard. It has 29 tricks split into two bands:

        • Easy band: surface tricks and first wake tricks, like the ollie, surface 90, surface 180, butter slide, wake 180, basic rolls and surface 360s.
        • Hard band: wake 360, heli, backroll, front flip, backflip, surface 540, tantrum, wake 540, surface 720, handle flip, mobius and wake 720.

        Badges mark milestones like your first trick, your first hard-band trick, ten different tricks, and landing every trick in a band.

        Trick skiing and write-ins

        If a trick isn't in the catalog, write it in. That includes trick ski moves, so a trick skier can keep a list of side slides, toe turns or wake flips by typing the names. The ski trick catalog with points lives in the calculator on this page for now, so write-ins are how you keep ski tricks in the app.

        A simple way to log a ski run today: write in each trick you landed, and add the pass to the name, like "Pass 1: side slide". Over a season, that list becomes your trick ski run log.

        Photos and clips

        Members of a club can share a photo or clip on a landed trick, and club photos stay private to that club.

        How to start

        1. Open the log and pick the kneeboard tab.
        2. Search for a trick or write in your own.
