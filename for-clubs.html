What members get

  • A club leaderboard: members ranked by their best results, with slalom and kneeboard shown separately.
  • Recent activity: what everyone in the club has been logging lately, plus each skier's own recent sets.
  • A slalom log: line length, boat speed and buoy count for each set, in mph or kph.
  • A trick run log: a calculator that uses the official IWWF point values. See the trick ski run log.
  • Kneeboard tricks: a list of tricks landed, with trick photos shared inside the club.
  • No install: it runs in any phone browser.

What the club officer does

  1. Request a club board with the form below.
  2. Share the link with members. Each member creates a free account, picks your club and asks to join.
  3. Approve who joins. Club admins approve or deny join requests and can add or remove other admins.

Privacy: members only

A club admin approves each join request before that person can see the roster. Only approved members see who's on it and what they've logged, and shared trick photos stay inside the club. Adults can add juniors, and those wait for approval too. Anyone trying lake.world without an account keeps their log on their own device only.

Calculators your skiers can use today

  • Slalom score calculator: pick the speed, rope and buoys to get the score.
  • Trick ski run log: two 20-second passes, official IWWF point values, no repeats.
  • Slalom pass log: track every set and watch your best pass climb.

Questions

Do members need to install an app?

No. lake.world runs in the browser on a phone or computer.

Can we try it before asking?

Yes. Open the log and try it without an account. That log stays on your device. Club boards need an account so the board knows who logged what.

Who can see our club's board?

Only members a club admin has approved.
