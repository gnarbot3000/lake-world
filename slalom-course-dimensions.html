Slalom course dimensions

An official slalom course is 259 m (about 850 ft) from the entrance gate to the exit gate. The first turn buoy is 27 m past the gate, the six turn buoys are 41 m apart along the course, and each sits 11.5 m out from the boat's center line, alternating sides.

The layout

Picture the boat driving a straight center line. It passes through the green pre-gate (where one is set), then the entrance gate, then between pairs of yellow boat guides past six turn buoys, and out the exit gate and past a second pre-gate. The skier enters through the entrance gate, goes around the outside of each turn buoy, and leaves through the exit gate. The turn buoys alternate sides, so the skier's path zigzags across the wake.

Official dimensions and tolerances

All values are from the Official Slalom Course diagram in the Appendix of the USA Water Ski & Wake Sports 2026 Official Tournament Rules (Rule 10.02 points to it). The letters match the diagram. Distances "out from the center line" are measured sideways from the boat path.

The two straight-line diagonals, C and D, are how a course is checked from the water. They follow from the other numbers: C is the diagonal of 27 m by 11.5 m, and D is the diagonal of 41 m by 23 m, the full width between turn buoys on opposite sides.

Buoy colours

The rulebook says gates and turn buoys should be orange (recommended) or red, and the interior boat guides are yellow. Record-capability tournaments, including Regionals and Nationals, must also have green boat path alignment gates (PG1 to PG4), and they're recommended at other events.

Home lake courses

A backyard or club course doesn't have to be tournament-legal to be useful, but the closer it is to these numbers, the more your scores mean when you ski somewhere else. If your course is short, narrow or missing gates, note it with your sets so you can compare like with like. Tournament courses have to be certified before the event, and the rulebook also allows two overlapping courses that share gates as long as each one is in tolerance.
