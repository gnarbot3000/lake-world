What a club board shows

When members log their sets in lake.world, the club board gathers them in one place:

  • Leaderboard: members ranked by their best results, with slalom and kneeboard shown separately.
  • Your recent logs: your own latest sets, so you can check what you entered.
  • Recent activity: what everyone in the club has been logging lately.

It turns a group text full of "ran 3 at 28 off today!" into a board everyone can see.

Privacy: members only

You pick your club and ask to join. A club admin approves each request before you can see the roster, so only members see who's on it and what they've logged. Shared trick photos stay inside the club too.

How to set up your club

  1. Create a free account. Club boards need an account so the board knows who logged what.
  2. Find your club and ask to join. An admin approves you.
  3. Log your sets. Use the slalom log for passes and the trick log for tricks.
  4. Check the board. See where you stand and who's improving.

Why clubs use a board

  • Newer skiers see steady progress, not just the top score.
  • Coaches can see who's stuck at a speed or line.
  • It runs in any phone browser, so there's no app for members to install.
